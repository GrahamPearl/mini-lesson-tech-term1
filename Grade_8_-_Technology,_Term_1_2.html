Grade 8 Pylon Design Reference

First pylon: 14 July 1928 · Bonnyfield, Scotland
Structure & Purpose

Frame structure built from steel or wood to support heavy electrical cables over a great distance.

A · Electrical Cables & D · Foundation

Cables (A) pull laterally and downwards. Foundations (D) anchor the legs to transfer compression and resist uplift.

C · Triangulation

Triangular shapes formed by members bracing a frame make it rigid and stable because triangles resist deforming forces better than rectangles.

B · Cross-Bracing & Visual Impact

Crossing members strengthen the frame and increase load capacity. Planners also consider visual impact and community safety around pylons.
